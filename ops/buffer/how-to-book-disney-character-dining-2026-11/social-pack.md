Want a Disney World character breakfast? Here's when you can book it.
• Dining reservations open 60 days ahead, at about 6:00 AM Eastern (5:00 AM Central)
• Staying at a Disney Resort hotel? Book your whole stay (up to a 10-night stay) at 60 days before arrival
• Most restaurants charge $10 per person if you cancel within 2 hours or don't show up
• Cinderella's Royal Table: no changes once booked, and full cost per person unless you cancel 2+ hours ahead
