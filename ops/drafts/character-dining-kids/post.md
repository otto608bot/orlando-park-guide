## Quick Answer: Which Character Meal Should You Book?

- **Princess fans:** [Cinderella's Royal Table](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/magic-kingdom/cinderella-royal-table/) inside the castle, or [Akershus Royal Banquet Hall](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/epcot/akershus-royal-banquet-hall/) in EPCOT for a lower breakfast price tier.
- **Mickey Mouse at a resort, no park ticket listed:** [Chef Mickey's](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/contemporary-resort/chef-mickeys/) at the Contemporary Resort.
- **Winnie the Pooh fans on a Magic Kingdom day:** [The Crystal Palace](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/magic-kingdom/crystal-palace/).
- **Stitch fans:** the breakfast at [ʻOhana](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/polynesian-village/ohana/) (breakfast only, characters do not appear at dinner).

## How Character Dining Works

Characters come to your table during the meal for photos and autographs, so you skip a separate meet-and-greet line. Meals are served one of three ways: buffet (Chef Mickey's, Crystal Palace, Tusker House, Hollywood & Vine, 1900 Park Fare, Cape May Cafe), family-style platters (Garden Grill, Akershus, ʻOhana breakfast), or a set prix-fixe menu (Cinderella's Royal Table, Topolino's Terrace breakfast). Story Book Dining at Artist Point serves plated entrées with shared starters and desserts.

Disney's pages note that character appearances are subject to change, so treat any lineup as likely, not guaranteed.

## Best Picks by Kid Age

These picks go by the meal format and the characters Disney lists, not by any secret ranking.

### Toddlers and preschoolers

Buffets and family-style meals let you start eating right away, which helps with little kids who can't wait for a plated course. Familiar faces help too:

- [The Crystal Palace](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/magic-kingdom/crystal-palace/): a buffet with Winnie the Pooh (Disney's page doesn't name other characters). Disney suggests planning about 90 minutes to see all the characters.
- [Chef Mickey's](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/contemporary-resort/chef-mickeys/): a buffet with Mickey Mouse (the only character Disney's page names). Each table gets an autographed keepsake card.
- [ʻOhana breakfast](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/polynesian-village/ohana/): family-style breakfast with Lilo and Stitch. Disney's menu lists Stitch and Mickey waffles.

For more on very young kids, see our [Disney World with a baby or toddler guide](/blog/disney-world-with-baby-toddler/).

### Ages 4 to 7 (peak princess and Mickey years)

- [Cinderella's Royal Table](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/magic-kingdom/cinderella-royal-table/): Cinderella greets you in the castle's Grand Hall, and Disney Princesses visit your table. Disney says kids are encouraged to dress up.
- [Akershus Royal Banquet Hall](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/epcot/akershus-royal-banquet-hall/): family-style Princess Storybook Dining in the Norway pavilion. Disney doesn't list which princesses appear.
- [1900 Park Fare](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/grand-floridian-resort-and-spa/1900-park-fare/): a buffet where Aladdin, Cinderella, Mirabel, and Tiana may appear.

### Older kids

Older kids often care more about the food and setting. [Story Book Dining at Artist Point](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/wilderness-lodge-resort/artist-point/) is a dinner with Snow White, Dopey, and Grumpy, and [Topolino's Terrace](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/riviera-resort/topolinos-terrace/) is a prix-fixe rooftop breakfast where Mickey, Minnie, Donald, and Daisy wear outfits made for the restaurant.

## Character Dining Outside the Parks

Several of the most popular character meals are at Disney resort hotels, not inside a park. Disney's pages for these resort meals don't list a park admission requirement, while in-park restaurants like Cinderella's Royal Table say valid admission is required.

- [Chef Mickey's](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/contemporary-resort/chef-mickeys/) (Contemporary Resort): breakfast and dinner
- [1900 Park Fare](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/grand-floridian-resort-and-spa/1900-park-fare/) (Grand Floridian): breakfast and dinner. Disney notes renovation work at the resort through early 2027.
- [ʻOhana](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/polynesian-village/ohana/) (Polynesian Village): characters at breakfast only
- [Topolino's Terrace](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/riviera-resort/topolinos-terrace/) (Riviera Resort): characters at breakfast only
- [Cape May Cafe](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/beach-club-resort/cape-may-cafe/) (Beach Club Resort): Minnie's Beach Bash Breakfast; no characters at dinner
- [Story Book Dining at Artist Point](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/wilderness-lodge-resort/artist-point/) (Wilderness Lodge): dinner only

A non-park day with a resort character breakfast is an easy way to give kids character time without buying another park day.

## How to Book Disney Character Dining

- **When:** Disney dining reservations open 60 days ahead at 6:00 AM Eastern ([planDisney, Oct 13, 2026](https://plandisney.disney.go.com/question/book-dining-reservation-cinderellas-castle-day-advance-646572/)).
- **Disney hotel guests** can book their whole stay, up to 10 days, at the 60-day mark. Everyone else books one day at a time.
- **No hotel or tickets needed to book**, per the same planDisney answer. You still need park admission on the day for in-park restaurants.
- **Cancellation:** restaurants charge a per-person fee if you don't cancel in time. At [Cinderella's Royal Table](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/dining/magic-kingdom/cinderella-royal-table/) you pay in full when you book, you can't modify the reservation, and you must cancel at least 2 hours ahead or the full price is charged.

## Character Dining FAQ

### Is Disney character dining worth it with kids?

It's worth it for kids who are excited to meet characters, because the characters come to you while you eat. If your kids are nervous around costumed characters, or you're on a tight budget, starting with one character breakfast keeps the cost down.

### When should I book Disney World character dining?

At 60 days out, at 6:00 AM Eastern. Popular meals can fill quickly, so be ready when your window opens.

### How much is character dining at Disney World?

On Disney's official pages (Oct 26, 2026), character breakfasts are mostly in the $35 to $59.99 per adult tier, and lunch and dinner are mostly over $60 per adult. Cinderella's Royal Table, 1900 Park Fare, and Story Book Dining are over $60 at every meal they serve.

### Which Disney character dining is best for toddlers?

Buffet or family-style meals with familiar characters are the easiest picks: The Crystal Palace (Winnie the Pooh), Chef Mickey's (Mickey Mouse), and ʻOhana breakfast (Lilo and Stitch).

### Can you do character dining without a park ticket?

Yes. Resort hotel meals like Chef Mickey's, ʻOhana breakfast, 1900 Park Fare, Topolino's Terrace breakfast, Cape May Cafe breakfast, and Story Book Dining don't list a park admission requirement on Disney's pages.

### Do characters appear at every meal?

No. At ʻOhana, Topolino's Terrace, and Cape May Cafe, characters appear at breakfast only, and Story Book Dining is dinner only. Check the meal period on the [character dining list](/character-dining/) before you book.
