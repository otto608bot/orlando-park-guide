## Is Animal Kingdom Good for Kids?

Yes — Animal Kingdom is one of the easiest Disney World parks for mixed-age families, because most of what it does best has no height requirement at all. Animal trails, a safari ride, a gentle boat ride, a train and live shows work for babies, preschoolers and grandparents alike. The trade-off is a shorter list of rides than Magic Kingdom, and only three attractions with a height minimum.

That makes height the first thing to check. ## Animal Kingdom Ride Heights at a Glance

- **Any height:** Kilimanjaro Safaris, Na'vi River Journey, Wildlife Express Train, Gorilla Falls Exploration Trail, Maharajah Jungle Trek, Discovery Island Trails, Conservation Station, Zootopia: Better Zoogether! (show)
- **38 inches (97 cm):** Kali River Rapids
- **44 inches (112 cm):** Avatar Flight of Passage, Expedition Everest

## What Changed in 2026: DinoLand Is Closed

If you are reading older guides, note that DinoLand U.S.A. is gone. DINOSAUR's last day was February 1, 2026, after TriceraTop Spin closed in January 2025 and The Boneyard playground in September 2025. The area is being rebuilt as Tropical Americas, which Disney has said is planned for 2027. Finding Nemo: The Big Blue… and Beyond! is a separate theater show; check the park's entertainment schedule for current times.

## Best Animal Kingdom Rides for Kids Under 38 Inches

Kids this age can do more of Animal Kingdom than any other Disney park. Start here:

- **Kilimanjaro Safaris** — an open-sided truck ride through a savanna with giraffes, elephants and more. No height requirement, and it is often the highlight for animal-loving preschoolers. Early in the day can be a good time to ride.
- **Na'vi River Journey** — a slow, dark-ish boat ride through Pandora's glowing rainforest. Any height. Some young kids find dark rides spooky, so preview it if your child is sensitive.
- **Wildlife Express Train** — a short train to Rafiki's Planet Watch and Conservation Station, a good indoor-ish break.
- **Gorilla Falls Exploration Trail and Maharajah Jungle Trek** — walk-at-your-own-pace trails with gorillas, tigers and birds. Easy to do with a stroller.
- **Zootopia: Better Zoogether!** — a seated indoor show in the Tree of Life Theater, useful for a midday reset.

For calmer kids, the ride finder's [calm Animal Kingdom picks under 40 inches](/rides/for/animal-kingdom-calm-under-40/) skips anything with drops or sudden motion.

## Rides for Kids 38 to 43 Inches

At 38 inches, **Kali River Rapids** opens up. It is a round raft that spins through rapids, and you will get wet — sometimes very wet. Pack a poncho or a change of clothes, and plan it for the warmest part of the day. Everything in the "any height" list still applies.

## Rides for Kids 44 Inches and Up

At 44 inches, two headliners open up:

- **Avatar Flight of Passage** — a 3D flying simulator on a motorbike-style seat. It is intense for some kids because of the motion, height illusion and 3D effects, even though it does not have drops in the coaster sense.
- **Expedition Everest** — a roller coaster with a backward section and a large drop. It is the park's biggest thrill ride; check your child's comfort with coasters first.

If one child is too short or not interested, Disney's **Rider Switch** lets one adult wait with the child while the other adult rides, then swap without waiting in line again (see Disney's Rider Switch page for current rules).

## A Simple Half-Day Plan for Kids Under 40 Inches

1. Arrive near park opening and head to **Kilimanjaro Safaris**.
2. Walk **Gorilla Falls Exploration Trail** nearby.
3. Cross to Pandora for **Na'vi River Journey**.
4. Take a seated break at **Zootopia: Better Zoogether!**
5. Finish with **Maharajah Jungle Trek** or the **Wildlife Express Train** to Conservation Station.

Older kids (44"+) can add Flight of Passage and Expedition Everest, ideally with Lightning Lane or at quieter times. Check current Lightning Lane options and prices in the My Disney Experience app, since they vary by date.

## Heat, Shade and Safety Tips

Animal Kingdom has long outdoor walks and Orlando summers are hot and humid. Plan shade and water breaks, bring refillable bottles, and use indoor shows to cool off. If a child shows signs of heat illness, get help from park First Aid or a doctor — Disney lists First Aid in every Walt Disney World theme park.
