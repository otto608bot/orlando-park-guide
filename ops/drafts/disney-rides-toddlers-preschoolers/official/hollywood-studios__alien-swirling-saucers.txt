An Out-of-This-Swirl Experience
The cutest extraterrestrials in the galaxy have landed, and they’re taking you for a ride. Power up the saucers for an interstellar romp through space, while futuristic tunes set the mood.
Space cadets of all ages may enjoy this gentle whirl through the sky above Toy Story Land.
It’s Screen Time!
Meet Lilypad from the Disney and Pixar film 
Toy Story 5
—alongside Woody, Jessie and Buzz Lightyear—in Andy’s backyard!
Lilypad is a spirited, frog-shaped smart tablet on a mission to connect friends. Plus, she’s using her tech to bring toys together for an all-new soundtrack to Alien Swirling Saucers.
Spin It Back with New Holiday Tunes
This holiday season, DJ Lilypad is debuting a playlist of fresh festive songs at Alien Swirling Saucers—Lilypad’s Jams. From November 4 to January 6, enjoy swirling with little green aliens while bopping along to new takes on popular seasonal songs, that include:
“Deck the Halls”
“Toyland”
“Swirling, Swirling Saucers”
“Joy to the Swirl”
