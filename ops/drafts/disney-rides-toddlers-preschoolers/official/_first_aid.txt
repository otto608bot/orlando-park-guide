At Walt Disney World Resort, your well-being is our top priority.
Theme Parks and Water Parks
If you begin to feel ill or find that you require medical assistance, please visit one of our conveniently located First Aid centers.
First Aid centers are available during normal park operating hours to offer over-the-counter medications, bandages and other quick remedies so that you can begin enjoying your vacation again.
Resort Hotel Services
In-room medical services are also available at all Walt Disney World Resort hotels. Simply touch “Front Desk” from your in-room phone for assistance, 24 hours a day.
First Aid centers can also store medications which require refrigeration. For your safety, special containers can be provided for disposal of hypodermic needles.
Offsite Urgent Care
AdventHealth Centra Care Urgent Care’s board-certified physicians provide adult and pediatric care.

Transportation to Centra Care Urgent Care is complimentary for Walt Disney World Resort Guests and can be arranged at First Aid Stations or by Resort Front Desk Cast Members. Additionally, you may call AdventHealth at (407) 939-7480. Guests younger than 18 years of age must have parent or guardian permission to call.
Emergencies
For all emergencies, dial 911 from any phone.
