Disney Fab 50 Quest
Help Tinker Bell spread pixie dust with your MagicBand+ during a quest across Magic Kingdom park, EPCOT, Disney’s Hollywood Studios and Disney’s Animal Kingdom theme park! 
Using your 
MagicBand+
, locate 36 golden sculptures—featuring 50 Disney characters—and earn Achievements when you find them all.
How to Play
When you approach one of the golden sculptures, your MagicBand+ will vibrate and its lights will spin and glow white. Then stand in front of the Disney character in the golden sculpture you located. While wearing your MagicBand+, move your hand in a circular gesture until your MagicBand+ vibrates and turns multiple colors—letting you know you have collected that character virtually!
