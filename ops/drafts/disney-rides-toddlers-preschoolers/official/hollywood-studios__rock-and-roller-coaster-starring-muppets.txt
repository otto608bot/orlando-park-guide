Get Ready to Rock… and Roll!
Step inside G-Force Records for a VIP tour of the Muppets’ music studio, where The Electric Mayhem is jamming out... and running very late for their own concert across town. Scooter (their band manager), Kermit, Miss Piggy and the rest of the Muppets must hatch a plan to get everyone—including you—to the show!
A Race to the Stage
Hop into a limousine and buckle up for a zany ride! You’ll zoom past the Muppets’ favorite Hollywood landmarks and swoop into 3 thrilling inversions—with 2 rollover loops and one corkscrew—on this roller-coaster-style attraction. 
Will you make it to the concert in time?
Tunes for a Rockin’ Road Trip
Speed along the freeway to some tunes from The Electric Mayhem—covering popular rock songs. Your limo’s audio system features the latest innovations from Muppet Labs, so you’ll catch every guitar-scorching note.
Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets: Obligatory Holiday Overlay
From November 4, 2026, to January 6, 2027, The Electric Mayhem is bringing 4 new holiday-inspired tracks to Rock ’n’ Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets, including collaborations with special guests.
This limited-time set list includes:
“Christmas (Baby Please Come Home)” by The Electric Mayhem ft. Miss Piggy
“Holiday Road” by The Electric Mayhem ft. Kermit the Frog and Fozzie Bear
“Little Saint Nick” by The Electric Mayhem
“Silver Bells” by The Electric Mayhem ft. Cyndi Lauper
Each song unwraps its own mix of holiday cheer, high-energy riffs and rock-star attitude.
About the Band
The Muppets’ legendary house band, The Electric Mayhem is beloved for their groovy tunes and rock ‘n’ roll attitude. Band members Dr. Teeth, Sgt. Floyd Pepper, Janice, Zoot, Lips and Animal all march to the beat of their own drum—and guitars, keyboard, saxophone and trumpet, too.
