Take Control of the Fastest Ship in the Galaxy
Hondo Ohnaka is looking for discreet flight crews to join Din Djarin, the mysterious Mandalorian bounty hunter, and his youngling Grogu on an exciting new mission—tracking down elusive ex-Imperial officers throughout the galaxy.
Ride in the famous cockpit of the Millennium Falcon on a daring flight—no experience necessary!
Plus, for the first time ever, you’re in control of the destination, leading to potential adventures on Bespin, the wreckage of the second Death Star around Endor or the bustling city-planet, Coruscant.
What to Expect
Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run is an interactive motion simulator attraction where your actions directly impact the outcome of your mission.
Whether you’re a pilot, gunner or engineer, every role is crucial. Each cockpit holds up to 6 crew members, with up to 2 per role:
Pilots
 – Fly the Millennium Falcon
Gunners
 – Fire at incoming enemies to defend the shipments
Engineers
 – Repair the ship, grab cargo and choose the destination
Find out if you have what it takes to outmaneuver the odds!
