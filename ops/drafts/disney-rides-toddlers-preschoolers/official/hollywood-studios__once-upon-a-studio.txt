First 100 Years of Dreams
Step inside this theater dedicated to honoring a century of Disney Animation storytelling. Enjoy the short film 
Once Upon a Studio
 featuring Mickey Mouse, Minnie Mouse and many more of your favorite Disney characters. But that’s not all! As you watch the film, be on the lookout for surprises along the theater walls, placing you right in the middle of this enchanting story.
Related Activities
The Magic of Disney Animation
Let your creative side soar in this reimaged courtyard where you can learn to draw popular Disney characters, pose for pictures and so much more.
Olaf Draws!
Learn how to sketch beloved Disney Characters with fellow animator-in-training, Olaf.
Off the Page
Pose for photos with popular Disney Characters and discover something new about the animation process.
