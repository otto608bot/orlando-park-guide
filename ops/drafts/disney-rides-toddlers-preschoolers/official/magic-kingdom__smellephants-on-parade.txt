Smellephants on Parade at Storybook Circus
When you enter Storybook Circus, see a Casting Call posted by Timothy Q. Mouse. He’s seeking a “Whiff-sistant” to join Dumbo’s act!
Once you decide to join, visit the ticket booth in front of Big Top Souvenirs. There, a Cast Member will provide you with an illustrated map of the land and a sticker sheet to guide you in your role as a Whiff-sistant.
Then it’s up to you! Find all 8 Smellephant statues scattered around Storybook Circus, using the stickers and map to track your progress.
Use your nose as your guide and enjoy the fragrances these colorful pachyderms are sporting—including popcorn, churro, cotton candy and more!
