Olaf Draws
Learn how to sketch beloved Disney characters at 
Olaf Draws
. Fellow animator-in-training Olaf is there to help you seek out renowned artists behind some of your favorite Disney animated movies. Together, you can learn tricks of the trade as you use pencil and paper to create a Disney animated character.
Off the Page!
Pan over to a stunning area where you can meet some of your favorite Disney animated friends. At 
Off the Page!
, you will step into different spaces—each highlighting a specific part of the animation process and offering incredible photo opportunities with a popular Disney Character.
Drawn to Wonderland
Watch your little ones let their imaginations go wild at Drawn to Wonderland. See them explore a playground inspired by Mary Blair’s brilliant concept art for 
Alice in Wonderland
. 
Disney Once Upon a Studio Theater
Settle in for a short film in which Mickey Mouse reunites with beloved characters and celebrates over 100 years of Disney animation. Be on the lookout at 
Once Upon a Studio Theater
—in a place like this, you can count on a little pixie dust to make a movie theater a little more magical.
