Bringing Day of the Dead to Life
You’re invited to “Remember Me!” – La Celebración del Día de Muertos, an exhibit that captures the beauty and spirit of the Day of the Dead holiday. One of the most important events on the country’s calendar, the celebration honors the dearly departed with parades, elaborate costumes and vibrant arts and crafts.
Tour the gallery’s intricately cut pieces of 
papel picado
 as well as calaveras, or decorative skulls made of sugar. See pre-Hispanic art that hints at the day’s beginnings and contemporary pieces that highlight its influence in modern Mexican life.
Don’t miss the iconic sculpture 
Bridal Couple
, who did not let death part them.
