Ready, Set, Sketch!
Step into a studio where Olaf, your cheerful companion, joins you in learning how to draw some of the biggest names in Disney animation.
Olaf welcomes you into a space inspired by one of his most memorable moments from the Emmy Award®-winning short film 
Once Upon a Studio.
 Go step-by-step through the creative process, guided by Disney Animation artists through video-recorded tips, techniques and stories from inside the Walt Disney Animation Studios. With Olaf’s playful humor the presentation becomes a fun, laugh-filled experience that is sure to leave you feeling inspired.
