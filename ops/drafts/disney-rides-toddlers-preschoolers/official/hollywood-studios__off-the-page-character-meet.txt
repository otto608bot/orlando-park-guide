Story

Mulan
Join the legendary warrior who proves anything is possible when you believe in yourself. 
Mulan encourages everyone to be true to their heart and embrace their own path. Her courage, skill and determination bring honor to her family. You don’t meet a girl like this every dynasty!
Find Mulan in the story department where you can see sketches from classic animated films.
Rapunzel
Have the best day ever with Rapunzel.
The creative and spirited Rapunzel is eager to experience the world. Her curious nature cannot be contained and her excitement for life touches everyone around her.
Visit Rapunzel in a space where dimensional landscapes showcase the layout department and Walt Disney’s invention—the multi-plane camera.
Chip ‘n’ Dale
Go nuts with everyone’s favorite inseparable fun-loving duo! 
Join the mischievous Chip ‘n’ Dale—these two always stick together and always have loads of laughs.
Catch up with these fun-loving chipmunks in an area that showcases the intricate skillset of traditional hand-drawn animation.
Donald Duck and Daisy Duck
Have oodles-of-fun with Donald and Daisy.
Adventurous Donald and his best gal Daisy are ready to share in a magical moment of laughs, friendship and all-around fun! They are ready to help everyone find their very own sparkle.
Discover the newest computer-graphic animation processes and styles when you meet Donald and Daisy.
Goofy
Step into the spotlight with Goofy!
A kind-hearted soul, Goofy sees the best in every situation! With endless charisma and charm, he can always be counted on to make things lighthearted and fun, making him the best pal to all who meet him.
Visit Goofy in the lighting department—where you’ll see a scene that changes from day to night.
Stitch
Say 
aloha
 and hang ten with Stitch!
Impulsive and fun loving, Stitch understands the importance of 
‘Ohana
. While sometimes silly, Stitch has still learned the value of friendship—and he always looks out for his extended family.
Join Experiment 626 in the Effects Department, where scene movement comes to life.
