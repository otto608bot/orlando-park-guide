An “Ear”-born Adventure
Based on the 1941 Disney animated masterpiece 
Dumbo
, this classic attraction lets you hop atop everybody’s favorite circus elephant as he discovers he can fly. Dumbo’s faithful friend Timothy Q. Mouse helps keeps you aloft with his “magic” feather. 
As the jubilant organ melody begins, lift off for a graceful cruise around (and around and around) Storybook Circus.
Fly High or Stay Low 
 You can adjust your altitude during your flight, so you can soar high or swoop low.
Play While You Wait
Before your flight, step into the big-top tent and let your wild animals frolic in an air-conditioned children’s playground for all ages. You’ll be alerted when it’s time to board your flight!
Smellephants on Parade
Embark on a brand-new, interactive search-and-sniff experience, featuring fragrances provided by Scentsy!
To join in the fun:
Start at the ticket booth in front of 
Big Top Souvenirs
 in Storybook Circus.
Pick up an illustrated map of the land along with a sticker sheet to guide you.
Find all 8 Smellephant statues scattered around Storybook Circus, using the stickers and map to track your progress.
There’s no cost to participate, so head over to Storybook Circus because Smellephants on Parade is fantastic fun for noses of all ages.
