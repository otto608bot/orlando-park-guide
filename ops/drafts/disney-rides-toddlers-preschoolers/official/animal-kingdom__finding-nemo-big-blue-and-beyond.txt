The Big Blue World – Live on Stage
Inside the cool confines of the Theater in the Wild, kids of all ages will be dazzled as a colorful underwater world comes to life with vibrant digital scenery, lively puppets and catchy songs from the composers of 
Frozen
, including fan favorites “In the Big Blue World” and “Go with the Flow”.
You may already know the story of the small clownfish from the reef who crossed the ocean to find his son—but you've never experienced it quite like this. Settle in as it’s retold by an unlikely group of fish—the Tank Gang from Dr. P. Sherman’s office! Now rescued and living at the Marine Life Institute, Gill, Bloat, Peach and the others recount Marlin and Dory’s adventure to find Nemo.
Finding Nemo: The Big Blue… And Beyond! is filled with music, humor, charm and wonder. It’s an experience the whole family will want to “just keep swimming” back to again and again.
