## Quick Answer: The Best Disney World Rides for Toddlers and Preschoolers

Most of what little kids love at Disney World has **no height requirement at all**. By our count of Disney's attraction pages on **November 16, 2026**, Magic Kingdom has 15 rides open to any height, EPCOT has 7, Animal Kingdom has 3 and Hollywood Studios has 2. The lowest posted minimum on any ride at the four theme parks is **32 inches**.

Here is the short version, park by park:

- **Magic Kingdom:** Dumbo the Flying Elephant, Prince Charming Regal Carrousel, Peter Pan's Flight, The Many Adventures of Winnie the Pooh, "it's a small world," Under the Sea - Journey of The Little Mermaid
- **EPCOT:** Frozen Ever After, The Seas with Nemo & Friends, Journey Into Imagination With Figment, Gran Fiesta Tour, Journey of Water
- **Hollywood Studios:** Toy Story Mania!, Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway, Alien Swirling Saucers (32 inches)
- **Animal Kingdom:** Kilimanjaro Safaris, Na'vi River Journey, Wildlife Express Train, the animal trails

## How to Read This Guide

- **"Any height"** means Disney's page lists no minimum height. Disney's pages are the final word, so also check the posted height at each attraction.
- **Heights are minimums, not readiness.** A child can be tall enough and still not love the dark, the noise or the motion. Where Disney's own page describes a ride as slow, gentle or mildly frightening, we say so. Otherwise we stick to Disney's description.
- **We don't map ages to heights.** Kids of the same age vary a lot. Use the height lists below and your own child's comfort level.
- **Rider Switch helps when your party is split.** Disney's [Rider Switch page](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/guest-services/rider-switch/) says adults can take turns riding while one waits with a child who doesn't meet the boarding requirements, then skip the standard queue on the second turn. Ask a Cast Member at the entrance.

## Magic Kingdom: Rides for Toddlers and Preschoolers

Magic Kingdom has the most no-height-limit rides of the four parks, so it is the easiest first Disney day for a short rider. Everything below is listed on Disney's official Magic Kingdom pages (checked November 16, 2026).

### Magic Kingdom rides with no height requirement

- **Dumbo the Flying Elephant** — Disney calls it a "whimsical flight aboard Dumbo," and you can adjust your altitude during the flight. The air-conditioned play area in the big-top tent makes the wait easier.
- **Prince Charming Regal Carrousel** — ride "atop a regal steed" past colorful scenes and music. A classic first ride.
- **The Magic Carpets of Aladdin** — fly over Adventureland on an enchanted carpet.
- **Mad Tea Party** — giant spinning teacups. Disney says you can turn the wheel fast for a wild ride or turn slowly, or not at all, for a gentle turn around the table.
- **Astro Orbiter** — pilot a spaceship high above Tomorrowland.
- **Tomorrowland Transit Authority PeopleMover** — a 10-minute tour of Tomorrowland, good for a seated break.
- **Peter Pan's Flight** — a high-flying adventure over scenes from the film.
- **The Many Adventures of Winnie the Pooh** — a ride through the Hundred-Acre Wood in an oversized Hunny Pot.
- **"it's a small world"** — a whimsical boat ride past a chorus of children from around the globe. Disney calls it a "gentle 10-minute journey."
- **Under the Sea - Journey of The Little Mermaid** — a clamshell ride through scenes from the film.
- **Buzz Lightyear's Space Ranger Spin** — Disney describes it as "dark, slow-moving and suitable for all ages." Kids fire a blaster at targets as they ride.
- **Haunted Mansion** — Disney says it is dark with "some mildly frightening scenes," but "no gore," friendly ghosts and a slow-moving ride. Preview it first if your child is sensitive.
- **Pirates of the Caribbean** — a swashbuckling boat voyage, also any height. Read Disney's page first if your child is sensitive to dark rides.
- **Jingle Cruise** — the holiday version of Jungle Cruise, any height (see the closure notes above).
- **Walt Disney World Railroad** — any height, on a modified shuttle route right now (see above).

### Magic Kingdom shows and play spots

- **Mickey's PhilharMagic** — a 12-minute 3D show starring Donald Duck. Any height.
- **Enchanted Tales with Belle** — you take part in Belle's fairytale alongside Belle herself, per Disney. Any height.
- **Monsters, Inc. Laugh Floor** — an interactive comedy show. Any height.
- **Casey Jr. Splash 'N' Soak Station** — a water play area for cooling off. Any height. Pack a change of clothes.
- **Swiss Family Treehouse** — a walk-through treehouse. Any height.

### Magic Kingdom rides with a low height minimum

- **32 inches: Tomorrowland Speedway.** Disney's page says guests at least 32 inches tall may drive their own car if they are accompanied by someone at least 54 inches tall.
- **35 inches: The Barnstormer.** Disney calls it a "junior roller coaster."
- **38 inches: Seven Dwarfs Mine Train and Big Thunder Mountain Railroad.** Both are coasters. Big Thunder is described as a fast coaster-type ride with some bumpy, dark parts.
- **40 inches: Tiana's Bayou Adventure.** Closed for refurbishment right now (see above).

## EPCOT: Rides for Toddlers and Preschoolers

EPCOT has seven rides open to any height, and most are boat or ride-through experiences. Heights below come from Disney's EPCOT pages (checked November 16, 2026).

### EPCOT rides with no height requirement

- **Frozen Ever After** — a boat ride through the wintery world of Frozen.
- **The Seas with Nemo & Friends** — Disney calls it a "gentle ride" based on Finding Nemo.
- **Journey Into Imagination With Figment** — a ride through the sensory labs of the Imagination Institute with a playful dragon as the guide.
- **Gran Fiesta Tour Starring The Three Caballeros** — Disney calls it a "gentle boat ride," and also describes it as a dark boat ride through the Mexico Pavilion.
- **Living with the Land** — Disney calls it a "gentle boat tour" past greenhouses.
- **Remy's Ratatouille Adventure** — a ride that makes you feel shrunk down to Remy's size.
- **Spaceship Earth** — a trip through the history of human communication.

### EPCOT shows and play spots

- **Journey of Water, Inspired by Moana** — a lush walk-through trail Disney describes as fun for all ages. Any height.
- **ImageWorks - The "What If" Labs** — a creative play space where, in Disney's words, "little ones can explore their senses." Any height.
- **Turtle Talk With Crush** — a real-time conversation with Crush the sea turtle. Any height.
- **Disney and Pixar Short Film Festival** — popular shorts in 4D. Any height.
- **Bruce's Shark World** (an interactive walkthrough exhibit) and **Kidcot Fun Stops** (activity-card stops across World Showcase). Both are any height.

### EPCOT rides with a height minimum

- **40 inches:** Test Track, Soarin' Across America and Mission: SPACE (Green Mission).
- **42 inches:** Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind.
- **44 inches:** Mission: SPACE (Orange Mission).

Disney says the Green Mission "doesn't spin" and uses a motion simulator with light movement, and it is the better choice if you are unsure which level to pick. The Orange Mission uses a spinning centrifuge. Kids under 40 inches can't ride either mission.

## Hollywood Studios: Rides for Toddlers and Preschoolers

Hollywood Studios has the fewest no-height rides: just two. It still works for little kids who love Toy Story and Mickey, and the heights climb quickly after that. All details are from Disney's Hollywood Studios pages (checked November 16, 2026).

### Hollywood Studios rides with no height requirement

- **Toy Story Mania!** — a 4D shooting game starring the Toy Story gang. Any height.
- **Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway** — a zany adventure through a cartoon universe. Any height.

### Hollywood Studios rides with a low height minimum

- **32 inches: Alien Swirling Saucers.** An "intergalactic spin" with the Toy Story aliens.
- **38 inches: Slinky Dog Dash and Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run.** Slinky Dog Dash is a family coaster, and Smugglers Run is an interactive motion simulator.
- **40 inches:** Star Tours - The Adventures Continue, Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance and The Twilight Zone Tower of Terror.
- **48 inches:** Rock 'n' Roller Coaster Starring The Muppets.

### Hollywood Studios shows for little kids

Disney's Hollywood Studios page lists these on November 16, 2026: **Disney Jr. Mickey Mouse Clubhouse Live!**, **For the First Time in Forever: A Frozen Sing-Along Celebration**, **The Little Mermaid – A Musical Adventure**, **Beauty and the Beast – Live on Stage**, and **Vacation Fun**, an animated short starring Mickey and Minnie at the Mickey Shorts Theater (any height). ## Animal Kingdom: Rides for Toddlers and Preschoolers

Animal Kingdom is more about animals than rides, and nearly everything it does best has no height requirement. Only Kali River Rapids (38 inches) and the two 44-inch headliners have a minimum. Details come from Disney's Animal Kingdom pages (checked November 16, 2026).

### Animal Kingdom rides and trails with no height requirement

- **Kilimanjaro Safaris** — an open-air vehicle tour of a savanna where you can spot real animals roaming free. Disney describes it as an 18-minute expedition. Any height.
- **Na'vi River Journey** — a boat ride into Pandora's glowing bioluminescent rainforest. Any height.
- **Wildlife Express Train** — a 7-minute, 1.2-mile train ride to Conservation Station. Any height.
- **Gorilla Falls Exploration Trail, Maharajah Jungle Trek and Discovery Island Trails** — self-guided walking trails with animals. All listed as any height.
- **Zootopia: Better Zoogether!** — a 4D show in the Tree of Life Theater with Judy Hopps and Nick Wilde. Any height.
- **Conservation Station** — reach it by train. Disney lists Animal Care at Conservation Station as any height, and the area includes Bluey's Wild World and the new Jumping Junction kangaroo and wallaby experience.
- **Wilderness Explorers** — a self-guided sticker-badge activity with more than 20 badges. Any height.

### Animal Kingdom rides with a height minimum

- **38 inches: Kali River Rapids.** A raft ride that can get you wet.
- **44 inches:** Avatar Flight of Passage and Expedition Everest.

Two big shows, **Finding Nemo: The Big Blue… and Beyond!** and **Festival of the Lion King**, are also on Disney's list. Check show times in the app.

## Which Disney World Park Is Best for Toddlers and Preschoolers?

Based on the any-height counts above, **Magic Kingdom** is the strongest first choice for a short rider because it has the most rides with no minimum. **EPCOT** is a good second, with slow boat rides and a play space. **Animal Kingdom** works well as a lighter day, especially for animal fans. **Hollywood Studios** is the one to save for later, unless your child loves Toy Story or Mickey, because only two rides are open to every height.

## Practical Tips for Riding With Little Kids

- **Pick two or three headline rides per park.** Fill the rest of the day with shows, play areas and snacks.
- **Use Rider Switch** if one child is under the height minimum (see the link above).
- **Watch the dark rides.** Disney describes Haunted Mansion, Gran Fiesta Tour and Buzz Lightyear's Space Ranger Spin as dark, and all three are any height. Dark is a different test than tall enough.
- **Plan for heat and water.** Dumbo's play area is air-conditioned, and Casey Jr. Splash 'N' Soak Station is a way to cool off. If a child seems unwell, Disney's [First Aid page](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/guest-services/first-aid/) lists First Aid centers at its theme parks and water parks.
