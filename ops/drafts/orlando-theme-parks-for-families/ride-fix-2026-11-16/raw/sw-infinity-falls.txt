Infinity Falls™
Rush the rapids on SeaWorld’s river ride Infinity Falls™.
Climb aboard your raft and prepare to get soaked while reaching peak excitement on Infinity Falls™, only at SeaWorld® Orlando. Wind your way around flowing fountains, surprise geysers and breathtaking waterfalls as you explore tropical ruins-themed surroundings with your fellow raft-mates. Then hang on as roaring world-class rapids send you flying through thrilling chutes and splashing turns before plunging you down one of the tallest drop of its kind into churning whitewater. While adventurers are almost certain to get wet, you’ll be able to dry off in no time at our family-sized dryers for a small fee.

Accessibility

Guests 48 inches in height or taller may ride unaccompanied. Guests between 42 inches tall and 48 inches tall must be accompanied by a supervising companion (at least 14 years old). Guests over 77 inches and under 42 inches may not ride. Mobility-impaired guests that would like to ride Infinity Falls­ should see the Team Member at the attraction main entrance.
