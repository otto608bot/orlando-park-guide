Sky Tower
Get a Bird's Eye View of Orlando
Our 400-foot Sky Tower has stood as an icon for our park since it opened in summer 1974. Today the Sky Tower adds a dose of retro-cool to the day’s adventures.

Get a seabird’s eye view of Orlando during this leisurely capsule ride, and take a load off as you plot the rest of your day in the park.



Platinum, Gold and Silver Pass Members ride Free!

Sky Tower requires a $6 (+tax) ticket for all regular guests.
