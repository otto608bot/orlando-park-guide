Rides & Attractions FAQs What are the height requirements for roller coasters at SeaWorld Orlando? Height requirements vary by ride to ensure guest safety. For example, thrill coasters like Mako, Kraken, and Manta require riders to be at least 54 inches tall, while Ice Breaker has a minimum height of 48 inches. Family rides and kid-friendly attractions are designed for younger adventurers with lower requirements. Be sure to check each ride’s guidelines in advance to plan the best experience for your group.
