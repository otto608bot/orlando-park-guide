For Good Measure
To ensure you have a vacation that’s both fun and safe, certain rides and attractions at our theme parks have restrictions based on your height. All attractions also have health and safety warning signs posted at their entrance. Make sure you read the signs while you’re in the park, and find out which rides are right for you and your family before you go. If you suspect your health could be at risk for any reason, or that you could aggravate a pre-existing condition of any kind, do not ride.

To see a list of requirements and accommodations for all attractions, please see the Rider Safety Page.
