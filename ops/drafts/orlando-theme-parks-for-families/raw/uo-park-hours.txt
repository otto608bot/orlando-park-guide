Universal CityWalk

Guests under the age of 18 without theme park admission (or separately ticketed events, as applicable) are required to depart at 8 p.m.* unless they are accompanied by a parent/guardian (21 years of age or older), staying at a Universal hotel, have a CityWalk restaurant reservation, have tickets for Hollywood Drive-In Golf or Universal's Great Movie Escape. Parent/Guardians must have a valid government-issues photo ID with date of birth to verify age. One (1) parent/guardian may accompany no more than five guests under the age of 18.
