LEGOLAND Florida Christmas Bricktacular is the Resort's annual Christmas celebration featuring festive LEGO decorations, holiday entertainment, seasonal treats, character meet-and-greets, and family-friendly activities. Guests can enjoy a giant LEGO Christmas tree, LEGO Santa, holiday-themed shows, and interactive building experiences throughout the park. The event takes place on select dates from November through December and is included with regular admission. Is LEGOLAND Christmas Bricktacular included with park admission? Yes. LEGOLAND Christmas Bricktacular is included with regular LEGOLAND Florida Resort tickets, Annual Passes, and select vacation packages. No separate event ticket is required, making it one of the best-value Christmas events for families in Central Florida. Can you meet Santa at LEGOLAND® Florida during the holidays? Yes. Guests can meet LEGO® Santa and several festive LEGO holiday characters throughout the event. Families can take photos, interact with holiday-themed LEGO characters, and enjoy seasonal meet-and-greet opportunities that create memorable holiday experiences for children. Does LEGOLAND Florida have a Christmas Tree? Yes. LEGOLAND Christmas Bricktacular features a massive LEGO Christmas tree standing more than 30 feet tall and built from hundreds of thousands of LEGO bricks. The tree is one of the event's most popular photo opportunities and a centerpiece of the holiday celebration. What makes LEGOLAND Christmas Bricktacular one of the best Holiday events near Orlando? LEGOLAND Christmas Bricktacular combines the magic of Christmas with hands-on LEGO fun, family-friendly entertainment, seasonal décor, holiday-themed attractions, and character experiences. Unlike traditional holiday events, families can enjoy all the rides, attractions, and festive experiences together in one destination. What else can families do at LEGOLAND Florida during the holiday season? In addition to LEGOLAND Christmas Bricktacular, guests can enjoy more than 50 rides, attractions, shows, MINILAND USA holiday displays, LEGO themed lands, SEA LIFE Florida Aquarium, and seasonal entertainment throughout the Resort.
