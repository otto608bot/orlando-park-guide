Bigger. Brighter. Merrier.
We're going all out with new festivities. Experience the Holidays at Universal Orlando Resort with Christmas in The Wizarding World of Harry Potter™ across three theme parks, Grinchmas™, Universal’s Holiday Parade featuring Macy’s, Snoggletog at How to Train Your Dragon – Isle of Berk, festive décor at SUPER NINTENDO WORLD™ and more.
Prices, times, dates, entertainment, event and/or experience details are subject to changes and/or cancellation without notice.
More Ways to Get Your Cheer On
Find tasty holiday food and drinks, shopping, character encounters and more around every corner of the Resort.
Seasonal Sweets and Treats
‘Tis the season to be hungry. Give your taste buds a present this year with exclusive food and drink offerings in the theme parks and Universal CityWalk—from seasonal snacks to holidays cocktails (21+ with valid photo ID) and more.
Seasonal Merchandise
From Christmas ornaments to hats, scarves and more, you can shop your jingle bells off throughout Universal Orlando Resort this season. There’s even a special Holidays Tribute Store at Universal Studios Florida for you to explore.
Capture Some Memories
Look out around the parks for seasonal decor and characters decked out in holiday outfits. You might even snag a selfie with one.
