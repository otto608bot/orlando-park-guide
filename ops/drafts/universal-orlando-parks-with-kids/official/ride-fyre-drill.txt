It’s a Soak or Be Soaked Sea Battle
With fire-breathing dragons everywhere, villagers have to be prepared. Viking twins Ruffnut and Tuffnut have transformed Berk’s Fyre Skööl into a watery, interactive boat battle at sea. Board your ship in Fyre Drill and blast your water cannons at flame-like targets – and the occupants of every other boat – to out-soak other Vikings.
Board your Viking ship and blast water at flame-like targets – as well as the occupants of other boats.
Fast Fact
Blast Away in a Watery Boat Battle
This watery sea adventure has water cannons that shoot over 28 feet through the air to reach the many targets throughout the ride, along with other Viking ships.
