Frequently Asked Questions
Expand All Collapse All
1. What is there to experience at Universal Orlando Resort?
Universal Orlando Resort includes four world-class theme parks and more: Universal Epic Universe : Discover our newest immersive park, featuring themed worlds like Celestial Park, Super Nintendo World™, Dark Universe and more.
Universal Studios Florida : Prepare for twists and turns as your favorite TV shows and blockbuster movies come to life at the Universal Orlando park that started it all.
Universal Islands of Adventure : Live thrilling stories, from a flying motorbike ride with Hagrid™ to the imaginative mind of Dr. Seuss and even hungry Velociraptors.
Universal Volcano Bay (a water theme park) : Brace yourself for gravity-defying plunges on the fastest water slides around or simply sink into tropical relaxation.
Universal CityWalk (a dining, shopping, movie and entertainment complex) : Bring it all together where dining tastes of every family member intersect with entertainment for all.
Universal Orlando Resort Hotels : Live it up with resort-style amenities, exclusive theme park benefits and hotel rooms close to the action.
Explore tickets for each of Universal Orlando Resort’s theme parks.
Create your own vacation package when you combine your tickets with a hotel.
2. What is Universal Epic Universe and how do I get tickets for it?
Universal Epic Universe is Universal Orlando’s newest theme park, filled with cutting-edge attractions and entirely new worlds like The Wizarding World of Harry Potter™ – Ministry of Magic, Super Nintendo World™, Dark Universe, How to Train Your Dragon - Isle of Berk and Celestial Park. It can be added to your Park-to-Park or multi-day ticket. Be sure to select a ticket option that includes Universal Epic Universe if you’d like to experience it during your visit.
3. What’s the difference between One-Park-Per-Day tickets (sometimes referred to as base tickets) and Park-to-Park tickets?
One-Park-Per-Day Ticket : Enjoy one day at Universal Epic Universe, Universal Studios Florida, Universal Islands of Adventure or Universal Volcano Bay.
Park-to-Park Ticket : Move freely between multiple parks in one day. Park-to-Park tickets are available at any Universal Orlando Resort theme park, including Universal Epic Universe. Guests with park-to-park access to Universal Islands of Adventure and Universal Studios Florida can also ride the Hogwarts Express™ between Hogsmeade™ and Diagon Alley™.
4. Do you save more by purchasing tickets with more days?
Yes! The more days you buy, the lower your ticket price per day. Universal Orlando Resort offers 2-Day to 7-Day ticket options with bundled savings compared to single-day ticket prices.
5. Are park tickets refundable or changeable?
Tickets are non-refundable (except with Cancel For Any Reason product if purchased and eligible), but most may be eligible for exchange to a different date. Please note that an additional fee may apply, as ticket prices vary based on the selected date. Please contact Guest Services for more information at  (407) 224‑4233 .
6. How do I choose the best ticket type for my trip?
It depends on your length of stay and interest in visiting multiple parks.
For short visits: A Base or 2-Day Park-to-Park ticket may suffice.
For full experiences: Choose from a 3-Day to 7-Day Park-to-Park ticket to take full advantage of everything Universal Orlando Resort has to offer, including Universal Epic Universe.
Universal Orlando Resort offers a variety of tickets as well as hotel and vacation packages, with pricing based on benefits and seasonality. Check our Deals & Specials Page for current offers.
7. What is the best order to visit the parks if I have limited time?
If you’re short on time, we recommend this general order to get the most out of your visit:
Start with Universal Studios Florida—great for immersive rides and movie-themed attractions.
Next, explore Universal Islands of Adventure—known for its thrill rides and adventure-packed zones.
Consider adding Universal Epic Universe or Universal Volcano Bay if you’re looking for something new or want a water park experience.
A Park-to-Park ticket allows for maximum flexibility when trying to visit multiple parks in a day.
8. What is Universal Express?
When you add a Universal Express pass to your theme park admission, you get to skip the regular lines at most of your favorite rides and attractions. It’s a great way to fit more fun into your day. Separate theme park admission required .
If you are a Guest staying at Loews Portofino Bay Hotel, Hard Rock Hotel® or Loews Royal Pacific Resort, your room key card will automatically be set up to grant you Universal Express Unlimited access at Universal Studios Florida and Universal Islands of Adventure (valid theme park admission required; select attractions; not applicable at Universal Epic Universe or Universal Volcano Bay). Each Guest will need to present their own room key card at participating attractions at either Universal Studios Florida or Universal Islands of Adventure to skip the regular lines. Express ride access is a separate line with a shorter wait time at each valid attraction.
9. Can I upgrade my ticket after purchase?
Yes! You can, in most cases, upgrade a Base ticket to Park-to-Park or extend the number of days. Visit Guest Services in the park or call Guest Services at (407) 224‑4233 to make modifications, subject to availability and pricing.
