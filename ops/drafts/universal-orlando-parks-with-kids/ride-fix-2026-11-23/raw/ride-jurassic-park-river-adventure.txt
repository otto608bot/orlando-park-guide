It's Lunchtime and You're on the Menu.
One minute, you’re gliding along in your raft. The next, you’re face to face with a giant T. rex. There’s nowhere to turn. She’s bearing down on you and your only escape from her razor sharp teeth is to take the pitch-black plunge before you. Did we mention it’s an 85-foot drop? Jurassic Park River Adventure is temporarily closed due to scheduled maintenance and will now reopen on December 17, 2026.

Your Complete Guide to Jurassic Park River Adventure
Jurassic Park River Adventure starts as a relaxing river tour through Jurassic Park’s herbivore containment area. Here you have a chance to come face-to-face with John Hammond’s (the original creator of Jurassic Park) prized creations.
