## Quick Answer: How Booking Works

- **When:** Disney World dining reservations open **60 days before** your date. New booking windows open at **about 6:00 AM** each day (Disney World is on Eastern time), per [Disney's dining reservation FAQ](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/faq/reservation-window/advance-reservations/).
- **Staying at a Disney Resort hotel?** At the 60-day mark before your arrival, you can book meals for your **whole stay, up to a 10-night stay**, all on the same morning.
- **Staying off-site?** You book each date when it reaches 60 days out, one day at a time.
- **Cancel in time:** most restaurants charge **$10 per person** if you cancel within 2 hours or don't show up. Cinderella's Royal Table charges the **full cost per person** unless you cancel at least 2 hours ahead ([Disney's cancellation FAQ](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/faq/dining-reservations/restaurant-reservation-cancellation/)).

Everything here was checked on Disney's official pages on **November 2, 2026**. Disney says its policies can change without notice, so re-check before you book. Still picking a restaurant? ## When Do Disney Dining Reservations Open?

Disney's rule is simple: you can book starting 60 days ahead. To find your date, count back 60 days from the day you want to eat. A few examples:

- **Friday, January 15, 2027:** book on Monday, November 16, 2026
- **Sunday, February 14, 2027:** book on Wednesday, December 16, 2026
- **Monday, March 15, 2027:** book on Thursday, January 14, 2027
- **Thursday, April 1, 2027:** book on Sunday, January 31, 2027
- **Tuesday, June 1, 2027:** book on Friday, April 2, 2027

That's plain date math. Disney's booking page is the final word, so if a date doesn't show yet, check again the next morning. Windows open at about 6:00 AM Eastern, which is 5:00 AM if you live in Central time. Disney notes that delays are possible.

## Resort Guests vs. Off-Site Guests

This is the biggest difference in how families book.

- **Disney Resort hotel guests:** starting 60 days before your **arrival** day, you can book dining for your entire stay, up to a 10-night stay. Disney points out that this lets you make all your reservations on the same day instead of checking day after day.
- **Everyone else:** you can book starting 60 days in advance of each meal date.

So if you're staying off-site and want a character breakfast on the last day of your trip, that date opens later than your first day. Put a reminder on your calendar for each one.

## Before You Book: Party Size, Tickets, and a Card

- **Count everyone, including babies.** For in-park table-service restaurants, Disney "highly recommends" a confirmed reservation for the total number of guests, regardless of age. Put toddlers and babies in your party count.
- **In-park meals need park admission.** Valid theme park admission is required to dine inside a park. A dining reservation doesn't guarantee park entry, and Disney says theme park reservations may be required depending on your ticket type. Compare tickets on our [ticket deals page](/deals/) before your 60-day window opens.
- **Resort meals are outside the parks.** For Disney Resort hotel restaurants, Disney encourages all guests to make reservations. Chef Mickey's, ʻOhana breakfast, 1900 Park Fare, Topolino's Terrace breakfast, Cape May Cafe breakfast, and Story Book Dining are all at resort hotels.
- **Have a credit card ready.** Disney's restaurant pages say the card you give when booking is charged a per-person fee if you don't show up or don't cancel in time.

## Disney Dining Cancellation Policy

From [Disney's cancellation FAQ](https://disneyworld.disney.go.com/faq/dining-reservations/restaurant-reservation-cancellation/) on November 2, 2026:

- **Most restaurants:** you can cancel or change online. Restaurants that take advance reservations don't allow changes within 2 hours of your time, and most charge **$10 per person** if you cancel within 2 hours or don't show up.
- **Cinderella's Royal Table:** once confirmed, the reservation **can't be modified**. To avoid paying the **full cost of the experience per person**, cancel at least 2 hours before your reservation time.
- **Check your confirmation email.** Disney gives each restaurant's policy when you book and in the confirmation email.

When someone cancels, Disney says the time goes straight back into the booking system for other guests. If the meal you want is full, keep checking. Spots can open up as plans change.

## A Simple Booking-Morning Plan

1. **Pick your top two meals per date.** Have a backup restaurant and a backup meal (breakfast or lunch) ready. Breakfast is often the lower price tier on Disney's pages, so it makes a good backup.
2. **Sign in the night before.** Make sure your Disney account works and your party is set up, including kids and babies.
3. **Be ready just before 6:00 AM Eastern** on your 60-day date.
4. **Book the hardest one first.** If Cinderella's Royal Table is on your list, start there, then move to the rest.
5. **Read the policy on each confirmation.** Note any meal with a stricter rule, like Cinderella's Royal Table.
6. **Didn't get it? Keep checking.** Cancelled times go back into the system right away.

## Character Dining Booking FAQ

### How far in advance can you book Disney character dining?

60 days ahead. Disney Resort hotel guests can book their whole stay, up to a 10-night stay, at 60 days before arrival. Everyone else books each date 60 days ahead.

### What time do Disney dining reservations open?

At about 6:00 AM each day, per Disney's FAQ. Disney World is on Eastern time, so that's 5:00 AM Central.

### Do I need to include my baby in the reservation?

For in-park table-service restaurants, Disney highly recommends a reservation for the total number of guests, regardless of age. Count babies and toddlers.

### What is the Disney dining cancellation fee?

Most restaurants charge $10 per person if you cancel within 2 hours of your reservation or don't show up. Cinderella's Royal Table charges the full cost per person unless you cancel at least 2 hours before.

### Can I change my Cinderella's Royal Table reservation?

No. Disney's FAQ says that once it's confirmed, the reservation can't be modified. You would need to cancel (at least 2 hours ahead to avoid the fee) and book again if a new time is available.

### Do I need a park ticket for character dining?

For meals inside a park, yes. Valid theme park admission is required. Resort hotel character meals like Chef Mickey's, ʻOhana breakfast, and Cape May Cafe breakfast are outside the parks.

### Can I call to book Disney dining?

Disney asks guests to book dining online. For extra help with an existing reservation, its cancellation FAQ lists (407) WDW-DINE, or (407) 939-3463. Guests under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission to call.
