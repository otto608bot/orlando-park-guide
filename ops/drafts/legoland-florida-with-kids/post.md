## Quick Answer: LEGOLAND Florida Rides by Height

LEGOLAND Florida is built for younger kids, and it shows in the height chart. Most rides open up somewhere between **30 and 42 inches**, and only two rides in the theme park need a child taller than that to ride: Mia's Riding Adventure (**48 inches**) and AQUAZONE Wave Racers (listed at **52 inches**).

- **No minimum height:** LEGO NINJAGO The Ride, The Grand Carousel, DUPLO Train, DUPLO Tractor, Jr. Driving School (ages 3 to 5), Driving School (ages 6 to 13)
- **30 inches:** Lost Kingdom Adventure
- **34 inches:** Coast Guard Academy, Rescue Academy, Safari Trek
- **36 inches:** Galacticoaster, Merlin's Challenge, Beetle Bounce, Royal Joust, Technicycle
- **40 inches:** The Dragon, Unikitty's Disco Drop, THE LEGO MOVIE Masters of Flight
- **42 inches:** Coastersaurus, The Great LEGO Race
- **48 inches:** Mia's Riding Adventure
- **52 inches:** AQUAZONE Wave Racers

Heights come from LEGOLAND Florida's official [height restrictions page](https://www.legoland.com/florida/plan-your-visit/know-before-you-go/attraction-height-restrictions/) and ride pages, checked on **November 9, 2026**. ## How Many Rides Are at LEGOLAND Florida?

LEGOLAND says the theme park has **more than 50 rides, shows and attractions**. That count mixes true rides with play areas, shows and MINILAND USA. Counting only rides with a vehicle or seat, our count from the official list is about 25, including the ones closed right now, plus the water park slides. For families with kids under 8, that's a lot: nearly everything on this page is open to a 42-inch child, and most of it to a 36-inch child.

## Rides With No Minimum Height

These are the easy wins for toddlers and preschoolers. Several still need an older rider along, so read the companion rule before you queue.

- **LEGO NINJAGO The Ride:** an indoor 4D dark ride where you "throw" fireballs at the screen with hand gestures. No minimum height; riders under 48 inches need a companion who is 14 or older and at least 48 inches tall.
- **The Grand Carousel:** a double-decker carousel in Fun Town. Kids under 48 inches ride with a responsible person 14 or older.
- **DUPLO Train:** a gentle train through DUPLO Valley. Kids under 34 inches ride with someone 48 inches or taller; handheld infants aren't allowed. Closed November 2 to 11.
- **DUPLO Tractor:** a slow farm ride. No minimum listed; kids under 42 inches ride with a responsible person 14 or older. Closed November 2 to 11.
- **Jr. Driving School (ages 3 to 5) and Driving School (ages 6 to 13):** kids drive their own little car around a course. These go by age, not height.

## 30 to 36 Inches: Where Most of the Park Opens Up

- **Lost Kingdom Adventure (30 inches):** a slow indoor ride where you shoot laser blasters at targets for points. Its ride page says riders 30 to 48 inches go with a responsible rider who is at least 14 and 48 inches tall.
- **Coast Guard Academy (34 inches):** kids steer their own boat. Riders 34 to 48 inches need a responsible rider 14 or older in the boat.
- **Rescue Academy (34 inches):** families take control of a fire truck and race to put out a "fire." Same 34-to-48-inch companion rule.
- **Safari Trek (34 inches):** a gentle safari ride past LEGO animals. The ride page says riders under 48 inches go with someone 14 or older.
- **Galacticoaster (36 inches):** the newest ride, an indoor family coaster in LEGO Galaxy where you design your own spacecraft first. Riders under 48 inches need a responsible rider who is at least 13 and 48 inches tall.
- **Merlin's Challenge (36 inches):** a spinning train ride. Under 48 inches rides with someone 14 or older.
- **Beetle Bounce (36 inches):** a small drop tower that climbs about 15 feet and bounces down.
- **Royal Joust (36 inches):** kids ride LEGO horses on their own. It's for ages 4 to 12 only, so grown-ups watch.
- **Technicycle (36 inches):** pedal-powered cars that rise as you pedal faster. Riders 36 to 48 inches go with someone 14 or older. Closed November 2 to 20.

One to check at the ride: **Kid Power Towers**, where kids pull themselves up a tower and drop back down. LEGOLAND's height chart lists **30 inches**, but the ride's own page says **38 inches**, with riders under 48 inches going with someone 14 or older. Plan on 38 inches to avoid a disappointed kid.

## 40 to 42 Inches: First Coasters

- **The Dragon (40 inches):** a steel coaster that runs partly indoors and partly outdoors. Riders 40 to 48 inches go with someone 14 or older.
- **Unikitty's Disco Drop (40 inches):** a spinning, bouncing drop ride. Riders 40 to 48 inches go with a companion who is 14 or older and 48 inches tall.
- **THE LEGO MOVIE Masters of Flight (40 inches):** an indoor flying-theater ride. Riders 40 to 52 inches must sit next to a companion who is 14 or older and 52 inches tall.
- **Coastersaurus (42 inches):** a wooden family coaster that dips around LEGO dinosaurs. A good next step after The Dragon.
- **The Great LEGO Race (42 inches):** a road-rally themed coaster. Riders 42 to 47 inches ride with someone 14 or older and 48 inches tall; 48 inches and up can ride alone.

See them together in the [LEGOLAND 44-inch ride list](/rides/for/legoland-44/).

## 48 Inches and Up

- **Mia's Riding Adventure (48 inches):** a disc coaster in Heartlake City. For most families, this is the park's biggest thrill.
- **AQUAZONE Wave Racers (52 inches):** you race through the waves in LEGO Technic vehicles and dodge water blasts. LEGOLAND lists a 52-inch minimum but also says riders 40 to 52 inches must ride with someone 14 or older in the same vehicle. That wording is unclear, so ask the ride host before you queue with a shorter child.

## Water Park Heights (When It Reopens)

The water park is closed for the season as of November 9, 2026. When it's open, it needs a same-day LEGOLAND theme park + water park ticket. Two slide minimums to know: **Twin Chasers, 42 inches** (under 48 inches rides with someone over 48 inches) and **Splash Out, 48 inches**. Joker Soaker's slides need **40 inches**, and DUPLO Splash Safari is for kids 6 and under with an adult.

If you're going over the holidays, this is the timely part:

- **Kids' New Year's Eve** is December 31, with an early "midnight" countdown and fireworks so little kids don't miss bedtime. It's included with general admission.

## A Simple Plan by Age

**Hours first:** LEGOLAND says the theme park usually opens at **10 AM**, closing around 5 PM on weekdays and 6 PM on weekends. LEGOLAND says event hours are posted closer to the dates. Check the [official hours calendar](https://www.legoland.com/florida/plan-your-visit/planning-tools/opening-hours/) for your date. LEGOLAND says Tuesday to Thursday is usually less crowded.

**Under 36 inches:** start with LEGO NINJAGO The Ride and Lost Kingdom Adventure (30 inches) while lines are short. Then do DUPLO Valley (Train, Tractor and the splash area), The Grand Carousel and Jr. Driving School if your child is 3 to 5. Add the 34-inch rides (Coast Guard Academy, Rescue Academy, Safari Trek) if your child clears that mark. Plan a midday break; a slower day beats a meltdown.

**36 to 41 inches:** go straight to Galacticoaster at opening, since it's the newest ride. Then Merlin's Challenge, Beetle Bounce and Royal Joust. At 40 inches, add The Dragon and Unikitty's Disco Drop. Fill gaps with MINILAND USA, a walk-through of American cities built from more than 32 million LEGO bricks.

**42 to 47 inches:** do Galacticoaster, then Coastersaurus and The Great LEGO Race back to back, then The Dragon. Save Driving School (ages 6 to 13) for the afternoon. Mia's Riding Adventure is still off-limits under 48 inches, so don't promise it.

**48 inches and up:** everything in the theme park except possibly AQUAZONE Wave Racers is open. Ride Mia's Riding Adventure and Galacticoaster early, then repeat favorites.

## FAQ

### What is the height requirement at LEGOLAND Florida?

There's no single park-wide rule. Several rides have no minimum height, most rides open between 30 and 42 inches, Mia's Riding Adventure needs 48 inches and AQUAZONE Wave Racers is listed at 52 inches (checked November 9, 2026).

### How many rides are at LEGOLAND Florida?

LEGOLAND says there are more than 50 rides, shows and attractions in the theme park. By our count, about 25 of those are rides with a seat or vehicle.

### Can a 2-year-old ride anything at LEGOLAND Florida?

Yes. LEGO NINJAGO The Ride, The Grand Carousel and the DUPLO Train have no minimum height, but kids that small ride with an older companion. Lost Kingdom Adventure opens at 30 inches, also with a companion.

### What's the tallest height requirement at LEGOLAND Florida?

AQUAZONE Wave Racers is listed at 52 inches. Among the coasters and thrill rides, Mia's Riding Adventure is the tallest at 48 inches.

### Is the LEGOLAND Florida water park open in November?

No. As of November 9, 2026, LEGOLAND lists the water park as closed for the season. Check the hours calendar for its reopening date.

### Is Christmas Bricktacular included with tickets?

Yes. LEGOLAND says Christmas Bricktacular is included with regular theme park admission on its select event dates.
